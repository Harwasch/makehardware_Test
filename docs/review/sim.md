# Review — Simulation — the resonant link and the DAB transformer

`sim` · requested 2026-08-31 · branch `claude/wireless-charging-design-hf0aix`

Two ngspice decks, every point cross-checked against closed form. Three findings, and two of them need a decision from you. F14: the white paper's coils cannot transfer 3 kW, and not marginally — 3 kW at 85 kHz between 400 V bridges needs a mutual inductance of 80.9 uH, the paper's coils are 12 uH, and mutual inductance cannot exceed self-inductance, so k would have to be 6.75 where k is at most 1. Simulated at resonance the v1 tank draws 403 A rms and puts 4.07 kV on the compensation capacitor against a 6.4 A device rating. This confirms the v1 baseline finding F8 by a different route and closes the question. F16 NEEDS YOU: the corrected ADR-0001 coil works, drawing 4 to 8 A across the whole docking envelope, but with a passive rectifier the output voltage is set by the load, and delivering 3 kW puts it at 471 to 622 V rather than the 400 V the HVDC rail assumes — and it swings 32% across the envelope at constant power. Either the rail is respecified and the vehicle bridge moves voltage class, or the receiver is actively rectified. That is an architecture decision. F17 NEEDS YOU: the vehicle DAB's loss budget does not close. ELE-003 allows 100 W for the whole converter and low-voltage bridge conduction alone is 76 W at four devices per branch, 152 W at the white paper's two. ELE-003's rationale was wrong — I had dropped the half-cycle conduction factor and understated both figures twofold — and it is corrected in the requirement, which keeps STATUS Draft and now carries evidence that it fails. What could NOT be simulated: the white paper names every semiconductor in the DAB and none of the three parameters that set its power transfer, so that deck solves for the transformer rather than modelling theirs. Neither deck carries switching loss, ZVS, dead time or parasitics.
